CLEM Divine Breakthrough Assembly is a vibrant, Spirit-filled church committed to transforming lives through authentic worship, sound teaching, and genuine fellowship.

Our Story

Born out of a deep hunger for genuine encounter with God, CLEM Divine Breakthrough Assembly was established with a clear mandate: to be a house of prayer for all nations. Over the years, we have grown from a small prayer group into a thriving community of believers, marked by signs and wonders, lives transformed, and families restored by God's grace.

Doctrine

What We Believe

Our faith is grounded in the eternal truth of God's Word — the Holy Bible.

The Holy Scripture

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God — the supreme authority for life and doctrine.

The Holy Trinity

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son (Jesus Christ), and the Holy Spirit.

Salvation by Grace

We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ — His death, burial, and resurrection.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the present ministry of the Holy Spirit, including the gifts of the Spirit for today's church.

Divine Healing

We believe healing is part of the atonement, and that God still heals the sick today in response to faith and prayer.

Baptism

We believe in water baptism by immersion as a public declaration of faith, and in the baptism of the Holy Spirit.

Holy Communion

We observe the Lord's Supper as a memorial of Christ's sacrifice and a proclamation of His death until He comes.

Second Coming

We believe in the personal, visible return of Jesus Christ and the resurrection of the dead unto eternal life.
